About Me

I’m Nandini Giri, an Associate Technical Consultant (QA) at Perficient with a strong foundation in both quality assurance and data analysis. I specialize in manual and automated testing using Selenium, Playwright, REST Assured, and TestNG, with hands-on experience in test case design, defect lifecycle management, API testing, and cross-browser automation. I have a keen interest in working with data — building interactive dashboards in Power BI, performing exploratory analysis with Python, and using SQL to uncover insights from real-world datasets. I believe the combination of QA rigor and data-driven thinking leads to building better, more reliable software. My recent work at Perficient’s GGTP program includes building full-stack test automation frameworks, designing 150+ test cases for e-commerce applications, and creating data-driven testing solutions. With a B.E. in CSE (AIML), I also have a strong interest in Generative AI and Agentic AI — I’ve built a Paper AI application using Neo4j and Agentic AI frameworks for research paper analysis. I’m passionate about ensuring software quality while leveraging data and AI to make smarter testing and business decisions.
